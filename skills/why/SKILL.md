# Why

Start from the current code, then inspect relevant commits, issue threads, PR discussion and changelogs when available. Separate original motivations, later compromises, and present-day behavior. Do not infer intent merely from the latest implementation.

Report what is directly supported by history and what remains inference. Convert findings into Preserve, Change, Avoid and Risk constraints before proposing modifications. If history cannot be accessed, say so and list concrete evidence needed.
